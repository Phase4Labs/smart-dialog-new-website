The problem

An answering service takes a message.
A chatbot makes conversation.
Neither understands your business.

Local and service businesses lose revenue every day because communication is scattered across phone, SMS, WhatsApp, and social media. Replies come late, intake is incomplete, follow-ups slip, and nobody can see the whole thread. Rango combines communication management, conversational automation, structured intake, lead tracking, and follow-up coordination in one system.

How it works

From "hello" to handoff, in seven controlled steps.

Every interaction follows the same disciplined sequence — no improvising, no dropped details.

Identify the channel & account

Rango verifies which business the interaction belongs to and that the channel is active and authorized.

Create or resume the conversation

Returning customers are recognized; new ones get a fresh customer and conversation record.

Determine intent

Estimate request, booking, emergency, availability question, or follow-up — Rango works out what the person needs.

Execute your playbook

It asks your qualification questions, collects required details, and never promises services or outcomes you haven't authorized.

Create structured data

Name, contact info, requested service, location, timing, urgency, qualification status — captured as fields, not free text.

Trigger the next action

Notify an employee, request approval, schedule a follow-up, route an urgent matter, or queue the lead for review.

Maintain the full history

Conversations, messages, leads, status changes, and automation activity all stay visible in your client panel — across every channel and every employee.

The playbook model

Your industry's know-how, tuned to your business.

Rango runs on playbooks, not prompts. An industry blueprint captures proven operating patterns for your trade — HVAC, family law, veterinary, childcare, landscaping, cleaning, and more. Your account playbook is generated from that blueprint with your services, service area, hours, qualification questions, and escalation rules.

  • Blueprints are versioned, validated, and simulated before publication
  • Rapid setup without treating every company identically
  • Your service boundaries and priorities, enforced in every conversation
Industry blueprint

Reusable knowledge and conversation patterns for your industry — tested and published centrally.

Account playbook

Your business-specific version: services, hours, service area, required fields, scenarios, escalation rules.

Execution

Every conversation runs against your playbook — intents, safety conditions, and business rules included.

Controlled automation

Automation without surrendering control.

Rango is not an unrestricted generative chatbot. AI interprets requests and holds natural conversations, but behavior is constrained by your playbook, channel permissions, business rules, and validation controls.

  • Clear line between automatic actions and those needing human approval
  • Sensitive commitments, scheduling, and exceptions stay in your hands
  • Routing protections: unresolvable interactions follow a sterile path — never another account's configuration or data
  • Isolated account context per business: your users, channels, leads, and data are yours alone

Client panel

  • Collected intake information and private internal notes
  • Notifications, delivery history, and recent activity
  • Connected channels, account, and playbook status
