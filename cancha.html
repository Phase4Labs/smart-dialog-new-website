The problem

Every missed DM is an empty court.

Players ask "got a court free this afternoon?" by phone, WhatsApp, and Instagram — and reception can only answer so fast. Booking apps add friction; phone tag wastes staff time; and the club never really learns who's playing. Cancha handles the whole journey in a channel players already use, and turns every reservation into knowledge the venue keeps.

How it works

From QR scan to confirmed match.

One conversation carries the entire journey — with real availability behind every answer.

Scan & chat

The player opens the conversation from a QR code — on the website, at reception, on the courts — or the venue's official WhatsApp number.

Instant recognition

Cancha knows returning players by phone number and greets them by name with their preferences. First-timers register once, in chat.

Book with real availability

Reserve, modify, or cancel against live court availability — date, time, court, and price, all in the conversation.

Pay either way

Online via Stripe — card, Apple Pay, Google Pay — with the court held for 15 minutes, or in person at the venue.

Confirm & remind

Instant confirmation, then reminders 24 hours and 2 hours before the match — on each player's preferred channel.

Bring the whole team

The organizer gets a share link: up to three companions can confirm attendance, register, and add the match to their calendars.

Everything lands in the panel

Reservations, court availability, customer profiles, approvals, refunds, and CSV export — a simple web panel built for the front desk, not for engineers.

Social monitor

Catch the bookings hiding in your comments.

"¿Tenéis pista esta tarde?" posted on Instagram is a reservation waiting to happen. Cancha's social monitoring spots booking intent in messages and comments and pulls it straight into the WhatsApp booking flow — and flags negative comments about courts, service, or facilities before they escalate.

  • Convert anonymous followers into known players
  • Capture reservations that today get lost in DMs
  • Reputation alerts with follow-up tasks for the team

Controlled automation

Real integrations, honest confirmations.

Cancha connects to the venue's booking manager and payment stack — it doesn't fake it. Where the booking system allows full read-write access, everything is confirmed automatically from WhatsApp. Where it doesn't, Cancha collects the request and reception validates before anything is confirmed or charged.

  • No reservation is ever presented as confirmed without a valid response from the source system
  • Refunds and exceptions stay under staff review
  • Privacy consent handled separately from marketing consent
